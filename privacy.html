Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 6, 2026

1. Privacy Principles

GymTimeTable was built with privacy as our core design philosophy. We believe that your health, routine, and training metrics are personal and should remain strictly under your control. We do not gather, store, or sell any information about your use of the application.

Offline-First Promise: GymTimeTable operates 100% offline. There are no remote databases, analytics integrations, account creation flows, or external API endpoints. Everything you input stays on your phone.

2. Data Collection & Usage

We do not collect any personal data or logs. Below is a breakdown of what happens to the details you enter inside the app:

  • Workout Logs & Schedules: All workout routines, weights, repetitions, muscle analytics, and scheduled workout sessions are stored directly on your local device.
  • Local Storage Technology: The app utilizes high-speed on-device MMKV storage. This is a key-value storage framework located entirely in your phone's sandbox directory.
  • Reminders and Alarms: When you set a daily gym reminder, a local Android alarm trigger is registered. No external server knows about or manages your schedules.

3. Third-Party Services

GymTimeTable does not incorporate third-party SDKs, tracking libraries, ad networks, or analytics tools (such as Google Analytics or Firebase). There are no cloud storage backups configured by default.

4. Permissions

The app requests the following system permission solely to support user-selected local features:

  • Schedule Exact Alarm (USE_EXACT_ALARM): Used strictly on your Android device to trigger daily reminders or workout alarm notifications at the precise time specified by you in the Settings panel.

5. Data Deletion

Since all data remains exclusively on your physical phone, you can delete your data at any time by:

  • Clearing the app storage or cache through your device settings.
  • Uninstalling the GymTimeTable application.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy occasionally. Because there are no accounts or email addresses stored, updates will be posted directly on this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed of our commitment to on-device privacy.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@gymtimetable.local
